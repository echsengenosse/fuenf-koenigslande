

>## Reghed Shaman
>*Medium Humanoid (Any Race), Any Alignment*
>___
>- **Armor Class** 11 (16 with *barkskin*)
>- **Hit Points** 27 (5d8 + 5)
>- **Speed** 30 ft.
>- **Initiative** +1 (11)
>___
>|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
>|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
>|10 (+0)|12 (+1)|13 (+1)|12 (+1)|15 (+2)|11 (+0)|
>___
>- **Skills** Medicine +4, Nature +3, Perception +4, Survival +4
>- **Gear** *Quarterstaff*
>- **Senses** Passive Perception 14
>- **Languages** Common, Druidic
>- **Challenge** 2 (XP 450; PB +2)
>- **Proficiency Bonus** +2
>___
>***Spellcasting.*** The shaman is a 4th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 12, +4 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following shaman spells prepared:  
>
>Cantrips (at will): *druidcraft*, *produce flame*, *shillelagh*  
>1st level (4 slots): *entangle*, *longstrider*, *speak with animals*, *thunderwave*  
>2nd level (3 slots): *animal messenger*, *barkskin*  
>
>### Actions
>***Quarterstaff.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:*  +2 to hit (+4 to hit with shillelagh), reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage, 4 (1d8) bludgeoning damage if wielded with two hands, or 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage with *shillelagh*.
