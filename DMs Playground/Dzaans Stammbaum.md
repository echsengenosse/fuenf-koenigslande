**Syriths** Name wurde aus dem Teppich gebrannt, ebenso wie der folgende Zweig, der ursprünglich bei seinen Kindern endete. Dzaan hat über die Jahre den Zwei rekonstruiert und selbst die Namen hinzugefügt.

# 1. Die Gründer
**Lord Khaezar Zhaendril** (Patriarch)
- A cunning necromancer who laid the foundation of the family’s power.
- Married to **Lady Eryndra Velmazh**, a transmuter experimenting on living beings.
- Children:
	- **Varick Zhaendril** (eldest)
    - **Syrith Zhaendril** (second son)
    - **Alvyn Zhaendril** (third son, dabbled in planar magic, disappeared in Limbo)
    - **Celyra Zhaendril** (youngest daughter, an enchanter who married into another Thayan family).

# 2. Die Eroberer
 **Varick Zhaendril** (Khaezar’s eldest son)
- Expanded the family’s influence.
- Married to **Selavra Dorneth**, a powerful enchantress.
- Children:
    - **Thaleon Zhaendril** (eldest, illusionist)
    - **Eryka Zhaendril** (evoker with a reputation for overkill in battle).
    - **Jadrek Zhaendril** (abjurer who specialized in warding the family’s secrets).
 **Syrith Zhaendril** (second son)
- Betrayed Varick, creating a rival branch of the family.
- Married **Mydris Talren**, a necromancer.
- Children:
    - **Eska Zhaendril** (eldest, evoker, died in a coup attempt).
    - **Velkor Zhaendril** (a warlock aligned with infernal powers).
    - **Alyndra Zhaendril** (diviner, vanished after foretelling doom for the family).
**Alvyn Zhaendril** (third son)
- Disappeared in Limbo; rumored to have sired children with planar beings.
**Celyra Zhaendril** (youngest daughter)
- Married **Tarn Velthur**, a conjurer. Their descendants split from the Zhaendril name but maintained ties.

# 3. Die Erfinder
**Thaleon Zhaendril** (Varick’s eldest son)
- Married **Lyris Marovahl**, a diviner.
- Children:
       - **Vorn Zhaendril** (eldest, warlock).

       - **Miris Zhaendril** (third daughter, abjurer who went rogue to join the Harpers).

- Children:
	- **Torvath Zhaendril** (eldest son, skilled artificer).

- No children (killed before passing on her legacy).
**Velkor Talren** (Syrith’s second son)
- Married **Thryne Oscalor**, a tiefling sorceress.
- Children:
    - **Myrrek Talren** (eldest, became a lich).
    - **Vyxis Talren** (shadow sorcerer with a penchant for assassinations).
