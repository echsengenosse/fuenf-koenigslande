# Sarven Zhaendril
_Medium humanoid (elf, shapechanger), neutral (with ominous leanings)_

**Armor Class** 15 (shadow ward)  
**Hit Points** 102 (12d8 + 48)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|11 (+0)|16 (+3)|18 (+4)|15 (+2)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|

**Saving Throws** Dex +6, Wis +5, Cha +7  
**Skills** Deception +7, Insight +5, Arcana +5, Stealth +6, Persuasion +7  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12  
**Languages** Common, Elvish, Draconic, Abyssal  
**Challenge** 7 (2,900 XP)

### Traits
**Shapechanger.** Sarven can cast _disguise self_ at will, but only to appear as a humanoid she has studied (Dannika, for example).

**Shadow Step (Recharge 4–6).** As a bonus action while in dim light or darkness, Sarven teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space she can see, and her next attack has advantage.

**Cunning Duelist.** Sarven is not trying to kill Xierran — the first time she would drop him to 0 HP, he instead falls to 1 HP, and she ends the fight with words, not blades.

**Innate Spellcasting (Shadow).** Charisma is her spellcasting ability (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit). She can innately cast:

- At will: _disguise self, minor illusion, thaumaturgy_
- 3/day: _mirror image, hold person, darkness_
- 2/day: _greater invisibility, phantasmal killer_
- 1/day: _dimension door_

### Actions
**Shadow Blade (Melee Spell Attack).** +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (2d6 + 3) psychic damage. Target must succeed on a DC 15 Wis save or be rattled by visions of their past (disadvantage on next attack).

**Umbral Grasp (Recharge 5–6).** Sarven summons shadowy tendrils in a 20-foot radius. Each creature of her choice must succeed on a DC 15 Strength save or be restrained until the end of their next turn. While restrained this way, creatures also take 7 (2d6) necrotic damage at the start of their turn.

### Reactions

**Shadow Veil.** When targeted by an attack, Sarven can impose disadvantage if she is standing in dim light or darkness.

### Roleplay Guidance

- She fights **defensively and stylishly**, retreating with shadow step, using illusions to confuse Xierran.
- Her goal is to **force him to reveal his strength and resolve**. If he shows mercy, restraint, or insight, she ends the fight with respect. If he’s reckless, she mocks him, slips away, and leaves a parting clue.
- She never fully drops the air of **“I know more about your father than you do”**.
